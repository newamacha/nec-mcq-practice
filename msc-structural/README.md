# IOE M.Sc. Structural Entrance Practice

Practice MCQs for the Tribhuvan University IOE M.Sc. entrance exam, Civil & Agriculture (CA) stream, the stream for M.Sc. Structural Engineering. There are 1,230 chapter MCQs across 7 chapters (English, Mathematics, Structural, Geotechnical, Water Resources, Transportation, and Water Supply & Sanitary) and 3 full mock exams of 98 questions / 100 marks each.

Tick an option for instant marking: a green ✓ when it's right, or a red ✗ with the correct answer and its explanation when it's wrong. Scores show both raw % and net marks with the exam's 10% negative marking.

Open it at **https://newamacha.github.io/nec-mcq-practice/msc-structural/**

Your answers are saved in your own browser only.
