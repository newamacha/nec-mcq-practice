# NEC Computer MCQ Practice

Practice for the Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) Computer Engineering license exam: 1,800 chapter MCQs (10 chapters × 6 sub-chapters × 30) and 5 mock exams of 100 questions. Tick an option for instant marking: a green ✓ when it's right, or a red ✗ with the correct answer and its explanation when it's wrong.

Open it at **https://newamacha.github.io/nec-mcq-practice/**

Your answers are saved in your own browser only.
